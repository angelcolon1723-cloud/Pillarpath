## Affiliate Disclosure

Some products and links in the Pillarpath Marketplace may be provided through
affiliate or partner programs. When you make a qualifying purchase through one
of these links, Pillarpath may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

We only surface products we believe are appropriate for families. Affiliate
relationships never influence chore rewards, classroom Units, or educational
content, and child-facing experiences always remain consistent with applicable
advertising and child-protection requirements.

A product is only described as an official partner integration when a real
business relationship exists.
